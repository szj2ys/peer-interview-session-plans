A handbook for peer facilitators

Run a useful interview rehearsal with the people and time you have.

Five complete session plans: exact roles, checked timetables, facilitator cues, examples, recovery options, and printable sheets.

Choose a format by group size and practice need. These are manual rehearsals with consenting peers, not employee-selection procedures. You need paper, a timer, and a true experience you can discuss without exposing confidential information. No account or live AI service is needed.

Choose one complete session

  1. 20-minute pair rehearsal without an observer

    Two people; each practises one answer

    Share the interviewer’s attention between listening and useful notes, while giving both people equal speaking time.

  2. 30-minute triad with an observer and role rotation

    Three people; everyone takes all three roles

    Separate interviewing from observation, then rotate through three complete eight-minute rounds.

  3. 35-minute panel rehearsal with bounded question ownership

    Four people; one candidate, one chair, two question owners

    Practise switching between listeners while preventing interruptions, repeated questions, and an unlimited question queue.

  4. 15-minute asynchronous voice-feedback exchange

    Two people; one candidate and one reviewer, working at different times

    Budget fifteen active minutes for one directed exchange; waiting for a reply is outside that budget.

  5. 12-minute one-variable retry with two takes

    Two people; one speaker and one listener for this session

    Repeat one answer with a single planned change, then compare what the listener actually understood.

Before the timer starts

Agree what the speaker wants a listener to understand, which topics to avoid, and how notes will be handled. Recording is optional and never the default. Anyone may decline a question. Feedback should distinguish words heard, the listener’s interpretation, and a possible revision.

The asynchronous plan budgets active work across one exchange; it excludes waiting for replies. The panel and retry plans give one person the candidate role. Run a separate session to reverse those roles. The pair and triad plans include their stated role rotations inside the advertised time.

Print or work offline

Open any plan and use its session sheet. The browser’s Print command includes the timetable and note boxes. Temporary typed notes are not sent or saved by these pages. Download all six HTML files and style.css together to use the handbook offline. Only optional related reading links require the internet.

Each format can stand alone. A useful outcome is one clearer answer, a more accurate listener understanding, or a specific next rehearsal—not a score, ranking, or hiring prediction.
