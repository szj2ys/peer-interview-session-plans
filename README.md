# Peer interview session plans

An original facilitator handbook with five complete manual rehearsal formats. Use paper, a timer, and willing peers; no account, installation, live AI service, or recording is required.

Published by the MakeInterview/MockLive team. These are manual peer-practice formats; the separate live AI interview service is currently paused.

## Pick the right format

| Plan | People | What the time includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `twenty-minute-pair.html` | Two | Both people answer, interview, receive feedback, and retry one sentence in 20 minutes. |
| `thirty-minute-triad.html` | Three | Three eight-minute rounds plus setup/debrief; each person is candidate, interviewer, and observer once. |
| `thirty-five-minute-panel.html` | Four | One candidate, one chair, and two question owners; a separate session is needed to rotate the candidate. |
| `fifteen-minute-asynchronous.html` | Two | One directed voice or text exchange, budgeted as 15 active work minutes across stages. Waiting is excluded; reverse exchange needs another session. |
| `twelve-minute-one-variable-retry.html` | Two | One speaker gives two takes with one planned revision; the listener compares understanding. Roles do not rotate inside 12 minutes. |

Every plan includes exact roles, opening cues, a timetable whose intervals sum to the advertised duration, a small fictional example, feedback prompts and a bounded feedback example, contingencies, debrief, constraints, and a printable session sheet. Participants use their own true, non-confidential experience. These formats are practice aids, not employee selection, rankings, language certification, or hiring-outcome predictions.

## Offline and print use

Keep `index.html`, the five plan HTML files, and `style.css` together. Open `index.html` in a browser, choose a plan, and follow its timetable. All instructions work offline. Optional related reading links require the internet.

Use paper or type short notes in the labelled worksheet boxes. The pages do not send or store those notes. Reloading or closing can discard them; copy anything you intend to keep. Use the browser Print command for the timetable and sheet, keeping text within the visible boxes. Agree on note retention and recording separately with participants; recording is never the default.

## Facilitator boundaries

Ask permission for the chosen practice format and any recording. Let a participant decline a question or stop. Separate observed words from interpretation and proposed revision. Label uncertain wording as a paraphrase. Do not turn a peer exercise into a hidden selection meeting or treat a second attempt as proof of improvement. The asynchronous format explicitly offers text instead of voice and asks peers to agree on recipient, purpose, deadline, and handling of copies before sharing.
